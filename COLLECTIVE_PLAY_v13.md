# Collective play — v1.3

Six integrated stages:

1. **Quick combinations and short play memory** — give-and-go and 1–2 touch sequences emerge from recent pass context.
2. **Passes into space and coordinated runs** — overlap, underlap, diagonal, depth, third-line and support runs.
3. **Third-man and multi-player chains** — 3–5 player combinations can emerge without a scripted move.
4. **Pressing triggers and defensive steering** — bad touches, safe recycling and second balls can trigger a jump, with explicit space-behind trade-offs; one-footed attackers may be shown toward the weaker foot.
5. **Shielding, body feints, physical duels and second-ball reading** — protection and contact alter the immediate situation rather than player attributes.
6. **Collective integration and rapid clock** — linked combinations consume less simulation time, so fast football is fast in the match clock as well as in narration.

Principles remain unchanged:
- no new numeric player attribute;
- no player-name exceptions;
- no score, winner, shot-volume or combination quota;
- same seed + same state remains deterministic;
- defensive pressure gains carry depth/space trade-offs;
- frozen v1.2 `main` is untouched.
