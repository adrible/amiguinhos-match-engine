# Continuous v1.3: pass-choice experiment and foul-contact geometry

## Decision

Retain the foul-location correctness fix. Reject the experimental aerial-pass valuation: despite fewer long balls, it produced more goals and fewer passes in a held-out paired sample, while the engine already overproduces goals. The rejected change is saved as `rejected_aerial_pass.patch`; it is not active in the engine. Its reconstructed engine SHA-256 was verified against the hash recorded before the test.

## Foul-location fix

The physical clock can move a player after a contact is recorded but before the referee handles it. The previous code decided the zone from the player's later coordinates, while setting the restart point from the earlier recorded coordinates. The new `_physical_zone_at` uses the recorded contact position for the foul decision. The position is the attacker's simulated centre at the contact tick; it is an approximation, not body-part tracking. Tests cover both directions and crossings of the front and side of the penalty area.

An independent exploratory audit recorded 262 foul decisions in 12 generic matches on the old engine. Median movement between contact and resolution was 0.16 m, the 95th percentile was 2.16 m, the maximum was 6.02 m and 32 incidents moved over 1 m. None of the 262 crossed the box boundary, so this sample does not establish that the location defect caused the excess penalties. It also does not address continued movement between contact and referee processing. The observations are retained in `contact_audit.json`.

## Experiments

There were three versions: previous draft, geometry fix alone, and geometry fix plus aerial-pass valuation. A 24-match paired development set used fixtures 124–147 and seeds 140124–140147. A separate 100-match-per-version held-out set used fixtures 160–259 and seeds 142160–142259. Each pair uses identical fixtures, synthetic teams and seeds. Previously observed aggregate results from this league were available during development; the held-out seeds and fixtures are disjoint from this round's pilot, but the data do not constitute a new league. All 372 games completed without recorded failures.

The discarded pass experiment adjusted the expected value of a cross or long ball using the receiver's aerial skill, incoming speed, pressure and surface. It did not impose a quota on passes, shots, goals or penalties. Different action choices can cause trajectories to diverge even with the same seed; the intervals below are paired bootstrap uncertainty for this particular synthetic sample, not a statement about real football or all match setups.

Goals increased by 0.67 per match (paired bootstrap 95% interval [0.05, 1.31]); long balls decreased by 45.17 ([-51.80, -38.63]); passes decreased by 43.28 ([-63.36, -24.62]). Penalties changed by +0.08 ([-0.21, 0.38]); this does not establish a change. Real and engine event categories are not perfectly interchangeable. Generic teams and physics-derived assumptions limit external validity.

The paired change in penalties was -0.01 with bootstrap 95% interval [-0.06, 0.04]. The geometry fix is accepted for correct incident location, not as a demonstrated calibration improvement. The system remains experimental, with far too many goals and penalties and too few completed passes compared with this reference.

## Reproduction and verification

Condensed match records include fixture IDs, seeds, per-kind pass counts, results and compact shot frames; they omit full tracking and full possession ledgers. Shard hashes and engine hashes are embedded in each compressed file. Both 100-pair summaries were regenerated byte-for-byte from these archives using `compare_paired_runs.py` and `compare_pass_types.py`. Hashes for the geometry-only engine and coordinate module are respectively `38b44ec6bfdd25f2413911e25b6e14408dbd3253a26b3a2a0328f0ec1a2187f4` and `1ad82058ade01e6fa24296f09e669b8483c953bd4c249ad0ac9d73457cea8129`.

178 selected local tests passed. One observer-parity match passed in each of the three held-out versions. Four synthetic matches of the retained version were saved at approximately 25, 40, 55 and 70 minutes; restored runs with observation produced the same full JSON-normalized final state. Four additional save/load runs of the rejected version also passed, but do not validate the retained version. These checks do not establish all-seed determinism.

Next investigation: record time spent in dangerous areas, contact opportunities by location, defensive decisions and longitudinal pass choices, then fit and evaluate against comparable provider events and, where available, tracking. The active fix does not recalibrate penalties, goals or passing. Do not promote this experimental engine; no official final seed was inspected or executed and `main` was not altered.
